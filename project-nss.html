Project Overview & Leadership Vision

As an active student leader in the National Service Scheme (NSS), spearheaded large-scale environmental sustainability drives across Tamil Nadu. The initiative united grassroots environmental action with structured youth mobilization to combat deforestation, strengthen groundwater reserves, and foster climate resilience.

Key Strategic Pillars

4.5 Lakhs+ Palmyrah (Palm) Seed Plantation

Strategically planted along riverbanks, lake bunds, and agricultural perimeters. Palmyrah roots penetrate deep into subterranean layers to prevent severe topsoil erosion and naturally recharge groundwater tables.

70,000+ Native Trees & Saplings

Planted resilient indigenous species including Neem, Banyan, Peepal, and Mahua to support local biodiversity, provide bird nesting habitats, and create green shade canopies.

150+ Miyawaki Method Mini-Forests

Engineered high-density urban micro-forest ecosystems that grow 10x faster and 30x denser than conventional plantations, creating rapid carbon sinks within institutional and community spaces.

1,000+ NSS Youth Volunteer Mobilization

Coordinated logistics, seed sourcing, community workshops, and volunteer teams across multiple districts in close collaboration with environmental bodies and local authorities.
